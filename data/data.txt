1. Working Days & Holidays
The company works 5 days a week. Saturdays & Sundays will be considered holidays. However, if the company organizes any presentation/training/employee engagement activities, the particular Saturday/Sunday will be considered as working days. The company will inform you well in Advance.
All employees for whom the company has arranged the presentation/training/activity will be required to attend the same. Failing to do so will result in a deduction of leave from the available leave pool.


2. Joining and Probation Period
The first 3 months from the date of joining is defined as the probation period. 
The Probationary period definition for new employees is the time between signing an employment Contract ( date of joining ) and being granted permanent employment status. It is the period during which the employee is evaluated as a suitable fit for a position in the company. 
The new employee will be given periodic feedback and guidance to have the chance to learn their new job and improve their skills during the probation period. 
The supervisor (reporting manager) will determine if the employee should be retained in the organization at the end of the probation period (or possibly before that). 
When a new employee completes his/her initial probation period, he/she will be granted permanent employment status. The company will issue an email ( or physical letter ) for successfully completing the probation period. There will be no provision for Paid leaves for the probationary employee.
The company may extend the probation period if required in certain conditions. Again, the same will be officially conveyed to the employee with feedback from the HR team and reporting manager. 


3. Performance Assessment and Salary Increment
For performance evaluation, the company is following the 360 Degrees performance appraisal method. 360-degree feedback or multi-source feedback is an appraisal or performance assessment tool that incorporates feedback from all who observe and are affected by the employee’s performance. 
In 360 Degree performance appraisal, the company is using the self-appraisal method and feedback gathered from the team leader/manager. For Self Appraisal, the company will provide the employee with an appraisal form to rate their own performance. Self-appraisal gives an employee a chance to look at their strengths, areas of improvement, and achievements, so they can rate themselves fairly. 
The appraisal cycle will be 6 monthly, till the employees whose experience is under 3 years. (For exceptional cases - Appraisal cycle will be discussed at the time of joining)
The appraisal cycle will be yearly in case the employee’s total experience crosses 3 years. In case the employee shows exceptional performance, the company may consider them for 6 monthly appraisals even though their total experience crossed 3 years. This 2nd appraisal is special and to achieve that, the employee has to set high standards for their performance & must show consistency. 
The performance evaluation is KPI-based. Employees will be rated on their performance with different KPIs. An employee must achieve a certain score to be considered for the appraisal. The monetary appraisals & promotions are directly linked with the performance of the employee.
The KPIs for each designation and department will be defined differently
KPI will change as per the Department and Designation 
The employee shall not switch the job at least for 2 months from the date of receiving the appraisal or else it will be reverted.
❖ Appraisal Process : 
The employee shall be asked to fill up the self-appraisal form in the month their appraisal is due. The employee is expected to share the updated self-appraisal form within 2 working days from the day he/she receives the form. In case if the employee does not revert with an updated self-appraisal form, the company may not consider them for the appraisal during that month. 
The complete appraisal process is highly confidential & employees are expected not to disclose their ratings to anyone except the reporting manager & HR. The appraisal form should be emailed back to hr@weservecodes.com within the stipulated time. 
The reporting manager will also be asked to fill in ratings for the employee whose appraisal is due. The reporting manager is expected to revert back with a filled appraisal form for the employee within 2 working days from the date of receiving the form. 
Also, the reporting manager has to ensure that the ratings given to the employee are fair and unbiased. The HR team will merge the evaluation form received from the employee and the reporting manager. The final sheet will be shared with the employee. 
In case there are noticeable differences in the ratings of the employee and reporting manager, the HR team will schedule a meeting with the employee and reporting manager to bridge the gap between the ratings. The final sheet & rating will come from the HR department, in case the difference in rating remains. 
❖ Performance Assessment Report: 
The final performance assessment report will highlight the strengths and areas of improvement of the employee. The employee should positively consider the report and should focus on their shortcomings. 
The reporting manager can also provide a specific plan for improvement of the employee performance. The employee should work on the improvement plan and should show improvement in the next appraisal cycle.

6. Office Hours
❖ Managing Time in a Work Day : 
All employees should devote a total of 9 hours for the office excluding commuting time. 
From 9 Hours in the office, one should devote 30 mins to lunch. 
30 Mins for checking emails and writing daily updates by EOD. 15 minutes for email & 15 minutes for daily updates.
8 hours should be pure billable work. 
The billable hours do not include any other breaks to be considered. 
● Employees should devote continuous time to work rather than taking frequent breaks. 
● Please remember, more breaks while working means longer working hours & less work-life balance.
 ❖ For Full Working Day 
In order to be considered a full working day, employees must have logged in a total of 8.30 hours in Inside/Upwork Tracker ( or any other tracker ) with a minimum of 50% work productivity. 
The working hours and productivity matrices have been defined in the Work From Home policy. The same would be applicable to WFO as well. 
At any point, if an employee's work productivity or working hours fall short, the company may consider deducting the employee's salary. Before taking such steps, the employee will be notified by the HR Team & respective managers regarding their low working hours/productivity and will be given a chance to rectify their working patterns. 
Once a month, an employee is allowed to log off a maximum of 2 hours earlier and compensate for those remaining hours by the end of next week. 
Under certain circumstances (eg. serious illness, the sudden demise of close family members, accidents, etc.) Managers & HRs can take the final decision. 
❖ For Half Working Day 
To be considered a full working day, employees must have logged in a total of 4.30 hours in Inside/Upwork Tracker ( or any other tracker ) with a minimum of 50% work productivity. 
For half working days, employees are not allowed to compensate for the remaining hours by the next working week, unlike full working days. 
The rest of the guidelines remain the same for half a working day.


7. Paid leaves and holidays
The company provides 18 paid leaves during the financial year. That means, the employee will get 1.5 paid leave per month. 
The total number of leaves will be adjusted according to the date of joining for the employee. If the DoJ is between the
1st to 15th of any month, employees shall receive 1.5 leave for that particular month. If DoJ is later than the 15th, the employee shall receive his/her PLs starting next month. 
● Employees are requested not to seek any leave within the first 4 weeks from the date of joining. Under unavoidable circumstances, the company may consider providing leaves on LWP. 
● Employees are requested not to seek any leave within the first 2 weeks from the date they receive their new project. ● Employees will not be allowed to take any leave during the notice period. In case the employee has to take leaves, the notice period may be extended or the leaves will be considered as LWP. 
● If an employee is getting married, a maximum of 3 working weeks' leave shall be granted to an employee who needs to manage from the leave pool. 
Apart from this you will get 12 fixed festival leaves which are considered as “Holidays”. You can refer to EMS for the detailed list. 
❖ Applying & Approving the leave 
All planned leaves should be notified to the reporting manager and logged in EMS/Inside well in advance. If any leaves are unapproved or in a pending state, that means that the employee cannot avail of those leaves. The leave should be approved/rejected by the manager well within the timeline. 
The company encourages the managers to approve the planned leaves and manage the client's work using other resources. However, under unavoidable circumstances like the launch of a website, an important demo/presentation/important discussion with a client, the manager can reject the leave of an employee. If the leave is rejected, the employee shall not avail of that leave and should remain present at work. If not done the same may consider the leave without pay.
Any unplanned leave must be logged by the employee in EMS/Inside after availing of the leave. 
● Leave for 1 to 2 days should be applied before at least 1 week. 
● 3 to 4 days of leave should be applied for at least 2 weeks prior. 
● Leaves longer than 5 or more than 5 working days, should be planned well in advance and should be notified at least before 2 months. 
● Maternity leave can be applied at any time, however, the company expects the employee to inform the HR Team regarding the pregnancy so that in case if required, The company can start work on backup resources that can be arranged on time of leave of the employee. 
❖ Leave Carry Forward 
The company does not provide an option to carry forward the un-availed leave to the next financial year. The employees are eligible to get 18 leaves per year. Per Month 1.5 Paid leave will be granted and It will start with the Financial year. 
❖ Leave Encashment 
The company provides a leave encashment at the end of every financial year. Encashment is based on Basic Salary (ie. Basic*Number of leaves remaining) If the Employee is eligible for TDS deduction, then it would be deducted from the encash as well. Interns are eligible to get the encashment. if an employee resigns or leaves the organization before the probation period is completed - he/she is not entitled to leave encashment

17. Performance Improvement Plan
The company follows a well-established Performance Improvement Plan for those employees who are not able to perform at par. Under this plan, an employee is given a chance to improve his/her performance, skills, attitude, or behavior to meet the established expectation and the industry standard. 
The team leaders/project managers are expected to give timely feedback to their team members about their day-to-day performance, achievements, loopholes, and areas of improvement by way of Appraisal Reports, Personal Discussions, and Team Meetings as and when required. Hence, an employee is given sufficient time to improvise themselves. 
At any point in time, if the reporting manager raises concern regarding an employee’s performance, behavior, attitude, or the employee’s ability to perform at par, the manager can suggest putting the employee under a performance improvement plan. 
The HR team shall formally inform the employee regarding his performance and the duration for which the employee will remain under close observation. The reporting manager will provide a clear guideline/improvement plan for the employee and monitor the performance of the employee. 
If an employee is not performing at par technically, this plan will help the employee reach the desired level. 
At the end of the review period, the employee’s performance will be assessed and a final decision will be made on whether the employee can continue working with the company or the company has to end the association with the employee. 
At the end of the review period, if an employee is terminated by the company, the employee is still liable for the salary and all relieving documents provided that the employee’s behavior is appropriate with his/her colleagues & management. 
● How the Plan Will Work 
As mentioned above, the reporting manager will raise a request to put the employee under PiP. 
The employee will be given a chance to express his/her technical or non-technical issues causing the performance issue. 
The company and the reporting manager will provide all assistance if an employee is lacking some skills or technical knowledge. To improve the employee’s technical skills, the company will provide necessary tutorials, code base, videos, etc to the employee for reference. 
The reporting manager shall set desired minimum achievable goals for the employee to be achieved at the end of the review period. 
If the issues are not related to work productivity, the same have to be addressed amicably by the employee, the reporting manager & the HR Team. 
Under all circumstances, everyone has to behave professionally and must maintain office decorum. Everyone is supposed to show respect and remain humble during all conversations. 
The employee’s performance will be evaluated against the KPIs defined and mentioned in the “Performance Assessment and Salary Increment” policy. 
The employee who is under PiP will be given time of 3 weeks to improve. During the 4th and final week, employees’ performance will be reviewed by the reporting manager & the HR team. 
In case the employee is put under PiP due to technical reasons, he/she will be given a technical test to assess the performance. If the employee clears the test, his association with the company shall continue. Failing the test may result in termination of employment. In certain cases, the company may consider extending the review period. 
An employee can avail of benefits or performance improvement plans only once during their association with the company. 
Note: The performance improvement plan is only for employees who lack technical skills or have minor behavioral issues. For any serious misconduct or unprofessional behavior, the company shall not consider the performance improvement plan for the employee, and the company can consider terminating the employee with immediate effect. In this case, the employee shall not be eligible to receive any remuneration or relieving documents. 
Note 1: If the employee does not perform well enough even after coming out of PIP, employment can be relieved at any time. Note 2: PIP scheme is available to any employee only once during the entire period of employment.

1.Guidelines for Interviewers
This document aims at aiding, and improving the Interview taking skills of all the members of the Interview Panel of Plutus Technologies Pvt Ltd (referred to as "The Company").
An interview means a face-to-face, telephonic, or any other type of interaction between the interviewer and the interviewee. An interviewer is the one who asks the questions, (s)he is the controller of the interview. (S)he is the representative of the Company in front of the eyes of the Interviewee. The feedback given by the Interviewee about the Company has a lot to do with how(s)he is treated during the interview. The interviewee is the one who answers the questions, (s)he is the respondent usually, also known as a Candidate.
Interviews generally need preparation, for Interviewers as well. Interviews have a definite structure. Clear communication should take place during an interview. All interviews have a definite purpose familiar to the Interviewer and the Candidate / Interviewee.
Key Tips for the Interviewer:
Appearance – It says a lot about you and the Company. The Interviewer should always take care of general grooming, dressing, and overall appearance. Our appearance should reflect the culture of the Company. Casual is fine. But, Business casuals / Formals dressing is the safest option. 
Good Behaviour is like half the battle won: 
We all are from Software Industry, So???? We must behave in a soft manner to make it easy for our candidates to understand us as well. The candidate/Interviewee should leave on a happy note from the interview room and should be friendly and understanding. The good behavior of the interviewer enhances the company's goodwill in the job market by manifolds. We shouldn’t indulge in arguments unnecessarily in the interview room. We should perceive them as supportive people. Avoid distraction in the interview area. For example, A phone ringing in the background can distract the interviewer and interviewee.
Interview Format – Conservative v/s Interactive
In the distant past, Interviews were taken in the traditional way wherein, the interviewer and the candidate acted like the Principal–Pupil model found in a school.
Nowadays, good companies follow the friendlier – interactive way. In this modern approach, interviews are conducted like interactive discussions rather than in the old-school Question –Answer way. Like our Software Coding, our questions should be Logically ordered so that it sounds like a fruitful conversation or interaction.
Our questions should be in line with the Skills, Experience, and background of the Candidate as well as the requirement of the Company.
Time Management – Tik Tok Tik Tok
Managing time is one of the most important aspects of an interview.
We must plan our questions and control our interactions in a way that would complete the interviews in 35-40 minutes max. Some time-liberty is understandable in special cases or during practical tests like situations.
We should start with Greeting the Candidate, offering some water, asking if he found the location quickly, etc. Ideally, these conversations should be completed in 2-3 minutes. As said earlier, it will give the candidate a chance to relax. Moreover, it is proved that Water intake results in higher oxygen levels in our body ultimately decreasing stress, and resulting in good interview performance.
Thereafter, we must give a brief introduction of who we are, what we do, when it all started, what we plan to achieve, etc. This short introduction will occupy 2-3 more minutes of our Time-wheel.
Then comes the part for which this whole planning is being done – The Technical Part. Straight away, we must aim to conclude our technical round in 25-30 minutes in 95% of the interviews. Our logically sequenced questions (rather say, the flow of interaction) should be in line with the PPP Model of Plutus. We should precisely utilize this time focusing on the strength of the Candidate, and just "Noting Down and Moving Ahead" with the weaknesses of the Candidate.
Last Few Minutes: Role Reversal – Happy Ending
In the end, let's spare a couple of (or more) minutes to satisfy the candidate's curiosities. Always encourage the interviewee to throw some questions at you. Upon selection, a candidate should not feel (s)he was not told about a certain aspect of the job. End the interaction with some cheerful, positive words.
Preparation & Prior Planning – PPP Model of Plutus: PPP Model will help you a lot in controlling the interview and managing time.
The secret of being a good interviewer is in preparation. There are some key things we need to prepare before the actual interview takes place.
First of all, we must Review the Job Description. If we understand the Job Description fully, it will give us a Clear Picture of the Ideal Candidate.
